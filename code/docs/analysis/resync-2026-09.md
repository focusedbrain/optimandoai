## Appendix D — Method

- **Read-only analysis.** The exceptions are the A0 securing steps the Author approved: three WIP commits, two archive branches, one archive commit and the pushes. Detached checkouts were used to re-take baselines; the tree was clean and the run returned to integration afterwards.
- **Graphify.** The AST-only graph was regenerated (`code/graphify-out/`, gitignored) and used for navigation only. Every finding cites source or a commit.
- **Throwaway analysis scripts** lived in `%TEMP%\resync-2026-09\` and are not in the repo: the capture parser, the typecheck differ, the Damm fold computation and the constructed-reference probe (the last two import the repo modules read-only).
- **GM1** was read in place with `pypdf`, printing to stdout only. Nothing was copied.
- **Four read-only explorer passes** (invariants and T1; PSL and August tracks; rulings; spec inventory) were cross-checked against this report. Every correction they caused was re-verified at the cited lines, except where a row says "explorer pass" or UNVERIFIED.
